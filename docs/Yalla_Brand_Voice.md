# Yalla.House — Brand Voice Guide

> Derived from site metadata, `_business/branding/YH Branding.txt`, the public marketing pages, and the German-first translation files. This document is the source of truth for every marketing message Claude writes on Yalla.House's behalf. Keep it short, keep it sharp, update it when reality changes.

## 1 · Who we are

**Yalla.House is a German-first, flat-fee property technology platform that gives owners, hunters, and agents the tools to work together without commission.**

We are not a real-estate agency. We are not a discount marketplace. We are a piece of software that replaces the role estate agents traditionally played as gatekeepers — by giving the people who actually want a transaction to happen the tools to make it happen themselves.

### The one-line positioning we always come back to

> Eine Immobilien-Technologie-Plattform. Kein Maklerbüro.
> A property technology platform. Not an agency.

---

## 2 · Who we talk to

We design and write for three audiences. Every piece of content names which one it is for.

| Persona | What they want | What they fear | What we say to them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eigentümer · Owner** | Sell or rent their property without losing thousands to commission | "Will I cope without an agent? Will it take longer? Will I get a lower price?" | Show them control + tools + proven steps. Make the absence of an agent feel like a feature, not a gap. |
| **Suchende · Hunter** | Find the right home without paying broker fees or fighting through gatekeepers | "Will I miss listings? Will I get scammed?" | Show them direct access + clarity + protected workflow. |
| **Makler · Agent** | Earn fairly for the parts of the deal they're genuinely good at, without competing with discounters or doing unpaid grunt work | "Will this disintermediate me entirely?" | Show them paid, optional, modular work. Yalla is the platform agents work *on*, not *against*. |

## 3 · Mission & differentiators — the things to repeat

These three claims show up in almost every piece of marketing in one form or another. They are not slogans we vary for fun; they are the brand position.

1. **No commission.** Pauschal, transparent, einmal. Never percentage-based.
2. **Full control.** The seller owns the listing, the data, the timeline, the agent choice.
3. **Built on clarity, not commission.** Every fee, every step, every contract is visible upfront.

Together they form **the Yalla Code of Clarity** — our internal name for the five-rule promise we make:

1. Pauschal — flat fee, never percent.
2. Transparent — every cost shown before you commit.
3. Optional — every agent service is opt-in, never bundled.
4. Modular — pay for what you use, nothing else.
5. Vollkontrolle — your listing, your data, your decisions.

## 4 · Voice attributes — pick five, hold them everywhere

| Attribute | What it sounds like | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Direkt** (direct) | Short sentences. Action verbs. "Inserieren Sie heute." not "Wir würden uns freuen, wenn Sie heute inserieren könnten." | Corporate filler, conditional padding |
| **Klar** (clear) | Concrete numbers, named tools, real steps. "199 € Pauschale. Auf ImmoScout24 in 24 Stunden online." | Vague claims ("Spitzenleistung", "Synergien") |
| **Selbstbewusst, nicht arrogant** (confident, not arrogant) | "Sie brauchen keinen Makler für den Großteil dieser Arbeit." | "Wir sind die besten / die erste / die einzige…" |
| **Praktisch** (practical) | Templates, checklists, calculators, how-to. We show, we don't pitch. | Aspirational lifestyle imagery without substance |
| **Respektvoll gegenüber Maklern** (respectful to agents) | "Makler bieten echten Wert in Verhandlung und Verkauf. Wir trennen das vom Pauschalpreis." | "Makler sind überflüssig / parasitär / veraltet" |

The fifth attribute matters: we win because we cooperate with the parts of the agent profession that are useful, not because we trash everyone in it.

## 5 · Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Length | Example opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing site headlines | Bold, declarative | 5–9 words | "Verkaufen Sie ohne Makler. Behalten Sie jeden Euro." |
| Blog / SEO articles | Practical, expert | 1,500–3,000 words | "Eine Immobilie in Deutschland privat zu verkaufen ist 2026 einfacher als die meisten denken." |
| Onboarding emails | Personal, helpful | 80–120 words | "Hi {firstname}, hier ist Tarek von Yalla.House." |
| Transactional email | Functional, friendly | 40–80 words | "Ihr Inserat ist live." |
| Cold outreach | Specific, respectful | 60–80 words | "Hi {firstname}, ich habe Ihren Beitrag in r/Finanzen über Maklerprovisionen gesehen." |
| Social posts (LinkedIn) | Founder-voice, story-driven | 150–250 words | "Letzte Woche hat ein Eigentümer aus München sein Haus über Yalla.House für 720k verkauft." |
| Social posts (X / Twitter) | Punchy, often a single insight | 1–3 sentences | "Die durchschnittliche Maklerprovision in Deutschland kostet einen Eigentümer im Schnitt 17.400 €. Wir nehmen 199 €." |
| Customer support | Warm, patient, never defensive | As long as needed | "Danke, dass Sie sich melden — lassen Sie uns das gemeinsam durchgehen." |

## 6 · Word lists

### Words to use (German, primary)

`Pauschale`, `provisionsfrei`, `Eigentümer`, `Suchende`, `Klarheit`, `Kontrolle`, `transparent`, `selbst verkaufen`, `modular`, `ImmoScout24`, `Immowelt`, `Übergabeprotokoll`, `Exposé`, `Energieausweis`, `Notartermin`, `Bestellerprinzip`, `Käuferbonität`, `Wohnflächenberechnung`.

### Words to use (English, secondary)

`Flat fee`, `commission-free`, `owner`, `hunter`, `agent`, `clarity`, `control`, `transparent`, `modular`, `Rightmove`, `Zoopla`.

### Words to avoid

- ❌ "Disrupt", "revolutionise", "game-changer", "best-in-class" — empty
- ❌ "Just …" / "Einfach mal …" — undersells the work the platform actually does
- ❌ "Cheap", "günstigste", "Billig-Lösung" — we are not the cheapest; we are the most transparent and the most user-controlled
- ❌ "Estate agent killer", "Makler-Tod" — alienates the agents who use the platform
- ❌ "AI-powered everything" — we use AI where it earns its place; we don't lead with it
- ❌ "World-class", "best in the market" — say what we do, not how we rank

### Numbers we use as proof

- Average German agent commission: ~3.57% buyer + ~3.57% seller (or 7.14% total, varies by region)
- On a €500,000 sale → ~€17,857 per side
- Yalla.House flat fee: [to confirm — keep the number concrete]
- ImmoScout24 monthly active visitors: ~14M (cite when claiming reach)
- We always cite our numbers; we never claim "millions" without a source.

## 8 · House rules for everything I publish

1. **Always name the audience in the brief.** "This is for Owners considering selling a Berlin apartment under €750k."
2. **Lead with a number when one exists.** "Spare 17.400 €" beats "Spare viel Geld."
3. **One CTA per asset, never two.** Pick: book demo, sign up, read more.
4. **Localise to Germany first, English second.** Default locale is `de`. Never publish English-only.
5. **No metaphors that don't survive translation.** "Move the needle" → "messbare Verbesserung."
6. **Cite the source on every external claim.** Especially regulatory ones (Bestellerprinzip, fee caps, Spekulationssteuer dates).
7. **Show the dashboard, the listing, the email, the receipt — real product UI, not stock illustrations.** This builds the "we exist" trust that pre-launch brands lack.
8. **Founder-voice on LinkedIn, brand-voice everywhere else.** LinkedIn posts come from `Tarek Alsaleh, building Yalla.House`. Everything else is `Yalla.House`.
